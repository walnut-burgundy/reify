At dawn, a school of small fish moved across the reef looking for plankton. A shark followed the school into deeper water. When the fish scattered, the shark caught and ate several of them.

----

Several kinds of fish live near the rocky shelf, where they feed on smaller animals. Sharks also visit the shelf. The number of sharks depends partly on how many fish are available, because the sharks eat the fish.

----

The fish population grew during the warm season, and more sharks arrived soon afterward. Each shark needed enough food to remain in the area. As the sharks ate the fish, the fish population declined and some of the sharks eventually moved elsewhere.
